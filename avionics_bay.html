Avionics Bay Manufacturing

Precision Clamping Design • CNC Milling • Fixture Design

← Back to Projects

Project Overview

Designed and manufactured precision clamping solutions for the rocket avionics bay enclosure. Working with a team, we created a multi-part clamping system to securely hold shells, plates, and internal components together while maintaining a watertight seal for flight operations.

The Challenge

The avionics bay required:

  • Precision clamps to hold enclosure components under pressure
  • Watertight seal integrity during flight
  • Reliable fastening that could withstand vibration and thermal stress
  • Efficient manufacturing of multiple similar components

Clamping System Design & Manufacturing

Fusion 360 CAM & CNC Milling

Designed clamps in Fusion 360 and programmed CNC mill operations:

  • Optimized clamp geometry for load distribution
  • Generated efficient tool paths for CNC production
  • Programmed multiple operations from single stock
  • Achieved tight tolerances on critical surfaces

Packing Factor Optimization

A key achievement was condensing multiple clamp parts into a single CNC operation from one piece of stock. This packing factor approach:

  • Reduced material waste and production cost
  • Minimized setup time with single clamping
  • Ensured consistent geometry across all parts
  • Decreased production time by optimizing tool paths

Radial Hole Fixture & Secondary Operations

Laser-Cut Fixture Design

Created a custom fixture for radial hole drilling:

  • Designed fixture geometry for precise hole positioning
  • Optimized for laser cutting from acrylic or aluminum sheet
  • Enables repeatable drilling and tapping on manual mill
  • Quick-change design for efficient workflow

Radial Drilling & Tapping Operation

Using the custom fixture on the manual mill:

  • Drilled radial holes at precise angles and depths
  • Tapped threads for fastener installation
  • Maintained tight tolerances across all radial features
  • Repeatable process for consistent quality

Assembly & Integration

Collaborated with the team to assemble the complete avionics bay:

  • Coordinated clamp installation with enclosure shells
  • Integrated internal plates and mounting hardware
  • Verified watertight seal integrity
  • Tested assembly under simulated flight conditions

Results & Achievements

Project Outcomes

  • Successfully manufactured precision clamps meeting all specifications
  • Completed assembly across multiple components made from different materials
  • Delivered ready-to-fly avionics enclosure
