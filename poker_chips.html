Poker Chip Set Manufacturing

Passion Project • CNC Lathe • CAM & Hand Programming

← Back to Projects

Overview

A passion project with a friend to create custom poker chips for our weekly poker nights. The goal was to design and manufacture a complete chip set using CNC lathe operations, exploring different manufacturing approaches and materials.

Manufacturing Approach

After testing multiple manufacturing methods (waterjet, manual milling, laser engraving), we've settled on a streamlined two-operation CNC lathe process combined with laser customization:

Machining Demonstration

Watch the complete machining process that brings our poker chip design to life:

Operation 1: CAM-Programmed Roughing & Knurling

Starting from long bars of material, we use CAM software to program the initial operation:

  • Create cylindrical chip body with precise diameter and thickness
  • Add knurled pattern on edge for texture and visual appeal
  • Create recessed center area for inlay or engraving
  • Cut off finished chips from the long bar stock

Operation 2: Hand-Programmed Secondary Operation

The second operation is hand-programmed directly on the lathe for added details:

  • Additional chamfering or edge finishing
  • Fine-tuning any dimensional features
  • Creating specific groove patterns or designs
  • Customization based on denomination or material

Laser Engraving

Final customization and personalization through laser engraving:

  • Add denomination values and suit symbols
  • Apply custom designs or logos
  • High-precision marking without material damage

Multi-Metal Vision

The long-term goal is to create chips from different metals, with each denomination having a unique material and appearance. Starting with aluminum prototypes, we plan to expand to brass, steel, and copper to provide visual distinction and different tactile feedback at the table.
